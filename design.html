Analysis Framework

Independent from the Interview Protocol. Editing this framework does not change interview questions, historical transcripts, or prior case reports. Every saved version is bound to one named research project and topic.

Global platform standards apply automatically to every project.

Keywords remain exact participant expressions. Every code and theme must use one human-meaningful, coherent concept that another researcher can compare across cases. One everyday word is preferred; two or three words are allowed only when they form one natural phrase. Bag-of-words labels are rejected and repaired. Transcript evidence, report lineage, and prior versions remain preserved. The project-specific fields below add the research topic, inclusion/exclusion rules, and allowable conceptual categories; they cannot weaken these global standards.
