Automatic Individual Case Analysis

Analysis starts automatically when an interview receives its formal completion signal. Complete transcripts are processed one case at a time, from the earliest completion onward. Opening this dashboard does not start or control the analysis.

The sequence is evidence first: exact keywords are highlighted in the transcript and colour-grouped into participant-specific codes; codes are then grouped into participant-specific themes. C1 and T1 restart for every case and never carry a fixed meaning across participants.

Researcher Data Analysis Dashboard

Case workflow: read one transcript → attach its demographic data → create one individual report (themes, codes, keywords) → place only that case’s themes in Form 1 → researcher validation.

A theme is a concept that categorizes codes. A code is a categorized expression of keywords. The individual report preserves all three levels; Form 1 presents only the highest level for comparison and validation.

Explore the real interview analysis as one workbook with three named worksheets. Each transcript is first analysed separately into an individual case report: evidence and keywords are identified, grouped into codes, then grouped into themes. Worksheet 1 presents themes first for researcher review and links to each individual report and complete transcript. Worksheet 2 maps codes to themes, and Worksheet 3 maps keyword expressions to codes. Each stage can be downloaded to Excel, rearranged and grouped by the researcher, then uploaded as a traceable researcher-validation layer for the next worksheet. Themes are the broadest concepts, preferably one word, such as Work. Differences such as Overtime or Long hours belong under concise codes; fuller interpretations remain in the rationale or individual case report.
