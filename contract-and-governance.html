Contract boundary

The analytical parties are the researcher and the designated AI only. PLI infrastructure may transport, freeze, hash, store, retrieve, display, protect privacy, record objective provider status, prevent duplicate writes, and enforce researcher-approved workflow barriers. It may not evaluate, approve, reject, repair, rewrite, or replace the designated AI’s analytical work.

The separate Global Research Governance / Participant Privacy Rules remain the platform-wide governance and privacy specification.
