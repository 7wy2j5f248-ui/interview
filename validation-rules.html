Participant protection boundary

A system-analysis failure is not a participant failure.

No AI output, model judgment, parser, validator, numbering issue, database state, or worker failure may disqualify a participant, exclude a transcript, classify it as unusable, or permanently render it non-processible. Formal completion controls readiness for complete-case analysis only; incomplete sessions remain retained and reviewable as not ready yet. Human rights and the participant's right to be heard take precedence; system failures remain visible, system-owned, and escalatable to researchers or system experts.
